# Campus Wi-Fi Access

The campus provides three wireless networks.

- `eduroam`: the main secure network for students and staff. Sign in with
  your full campus email (`username@example.edu`) and your account password.
  This is the recommended network everywhere on campus.
- `Campus-Guest`: for visitors. Guests self-register at
  https://wifi.example.edu/guest and receive a voucher valid for 24 hours.
  Guest sessions are capped at 5 Mbps and block peer-to-peer traffic.
- `Campus-Setup`: an open network used only to onboard a new device; it
  reaches the setup page and nothing else.

## Device limits and support

- Each account may connect up to 8 devices to eduroam at once.
- For gaming consoles and smart TVs that cannot run eduroam, register the
  device's MAC address at https://wifi.example.edu/device.
- Coverage maps and known dead spots are listed at
  https://it.example.edu/wifi.

If eduroam rejects your login, first confirm your password works at
https://account.example.edu, then forget and rejoin the network. Persistent
problems go to the IT Service Desk at extension 4500 or helpdesk@example.edu.
