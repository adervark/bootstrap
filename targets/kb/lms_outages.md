# Learning Management System (LMS) Status and Outages

The campus LMS is CanvasEdu, reached at https://lms.example.edu. It hosts
course materials, assignment submission and grades.

## Scheduled maintenance

- Routine maintenance runs every Tuesday 02:00-04:00. The LMS may be briefly
  unavailable in this window.
- Major upgrades are announced at least 7 days ahead by email and on the
  status page.

## During an outage

- Check the live status page at https://status.example.edu, which is hosted
  separately from the LMS and stays up during LMS outages.
- If an assignment deadline falls during a confirmed outage, submissions are
  automatically extended by 24 hours from the time service is restored. You do
  not need to request this extension; it is applied by the registrar.
- Do not email your work to your instructor unless they ask; graded
  submissions must go through the LMS for the audit trail.

## Reporting a problem

Report LMS faults at https://it.example.edu/lms or by calling extension 4500.
When reporting, include your course code, the time, and a screenshot of any
error. The target response time for an LMS incident is 2 hours during
business hours.
