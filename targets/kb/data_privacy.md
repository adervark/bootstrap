# Data Privacy and Acceptable Use

The University processes your personal data under its Data Protection Policy.
This note covers what IT systems collect and your rights.

## What is collected

- Account and sign-in logs (time, system, and source network) are kept for
  12 months for security investigations.
- LMS activity (page views, submission times) is visible to your instructors
  for the courses you are enrolled in, and to no one else.
- Lab and print usage is recorded against your account for quota management.

## Your rights

- You may request a copy of the personal data IT holds about you by emailing
  dpo@example.edu. The University responds within 30 days.
- You may ask for correction of inaccurate data.
- Security logs cannot be deleted on request while a retention period or an
  investigation is active.

## Acceptable use

- Your account is for your use only. Never share your password or let someone
  else use your session.
- Do not use campus systems to store or share others' personal data without
  authorisation.
- Suspected data breaches must be reported to security@example.edu within
  24 hours of discovery.

Questions about privacy go to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@example.edu.
Questions about accounts go to the IT Service Desk at extension 4500.
