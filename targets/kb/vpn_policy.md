# Campus VPN Policy

The University VPN gives secure remote access to internal systems such as the
library databases, the research file store and the admin portal.

- Download the GlobalReach VPN client from https://it.example.edu/vpn.
- Connect to the gateway `vpn.example.edu` using your campus username and
  password, then approve the MFA push (see MFA setup).
- The VPN is required for any off-campus access to the admin portal and the
  licensed research databases. On-campus wired and eduroam connections do not
  need it.
- Sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours or after 30 minutes of
  inactivity, whichever comes first.
- Students may run at most 2 simultaneous VPN sessions. Staff may run up to 5.
- Split tunnelling is disabled: while connected, all traffic is routed through
  the campus gateway.

For help, contact the IT Service Desk at extension 4500 or helpdesk@example.edu.
