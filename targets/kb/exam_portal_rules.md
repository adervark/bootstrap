# Online Exam Portal Rules

Timed online exams are delivered through the exam portal at
https://exams.example.edu, which is separate from the LMS.

## Before the exam

- Log in and run the system check at least 24 hours before your exam. It
  confirms your browser, camera and connection.
- Use a supported browser: the current version of Chrome or Firefox. The
  portal does not support Safari or mobile browsers.
- Close all other applications. The portal uses a lockdown mode that blocks
  other tabs and screen sharing for the duration of the exam.

## During the exam

- A countdown timer is shown at all times. When it reaches zero, answers are
  submitted automatically.
- The portal auto-saves every 30 seconds. If you lose connection, log back in
  within 10 minutes and you resume where you left off, with the clock still
  running.
- You may not leave the exam window. Leaving it more than twice is flagged to
  the invigilator.

## Technical problems

If you are disconnected for more than 10 minutes or the portal fails, call the
exam support line at extension 4511 immediately and file an incident at
https://exams.example.edu/incident. Do not close the browser. Claims of
technical failure are checked against the portal's server logs before any
re-sit is granted.
