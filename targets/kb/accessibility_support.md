# Accessibility and Assistive Technology Support

The University provides IT accommodations for students and staff with
disabilities, arranged through the Accessibility Service.

## Requesting support

1. Register with the Accessibility Service at
   https://access.example.edu, which assesses needs and issues a support
   plan.
2. IT then applies your plan: extended exam time in the exam portal, assistive
   software on lab machines, or loan equipment.
3. Allow up to 10 working days for new software or equipment to be arranged.

## Assistive technology available

- Screen readers (NVDA and JAWS) and screen magnification on all lab machines
  on request.
- Read-aloud and dictation built into the managed Office install.
- Loan equipment: adjustable keyboards, ergonomic mice, and noise-cancelling
  headsets, booked at https://access.example.edu/equipment.

## Exam adjustments

Extended time and rest breaks are configured in the exam portal automatically
once your support plan is active. Confirm your adjustments show correctly
during the pre-exam system check; if not, contact the exam support line at
extension 4511 at least 48 hours before the exam.

For help using any assistive tool, contact the Accessibility IT team at
access-it@example.edu or extension 4520.
