# IT Services by Role: Students, Staff and International Users

Some IT entitlements differ by role. This page lists the differences in one
place; the topic pages give the full detail.

## Mailbox and storage

- Students: 50 GB mailbox and 1 TB cloud file storage.
- Staff: 100 GB mailbox and 5 TB cloud file storage, plus a shared
  departmental drive.
- Alumni keep a forwarding-only mailbox for 2 years after graduation; no file
  storage.

## Security requirements

- Students and staff both use MFA.
- Staff who handle student records must use a hardware security key as their
  primary MFA factor.
- Student passwords expire every 180 days; staff passwords every 90 days.

## Remote access

- Students may run 2 VPN sessions at once; staff may run 5.
- International students connecting from outside the country must re-verify a
  recovery email on their first password reset each term, and may see an extra
  identity check when enrolling a new device.

## Support hours

- The IT Service Desk (extension 4500) serves everyone Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00.
- Staff supporting live teaching have an after-hours priority line, extension
  4501, open until 22:00 on weekdays.
- Students use the 24-hour self-service portal and community forum outside
  Service Desk hours.

If you are unsure which entitlement applies to you, the Service Desk can
confirm your role on request.
