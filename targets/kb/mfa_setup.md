# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA protects your campus account with a second factor in addition to your
password. It is mandatory for all students and staff.

## Enrolling

1. Install the CampusAuth app from your phone's app store.
2. Sign in at https://mfa.example.edu/enroll and scan the QR code shown.
3. Register at least one backup method: an authenticator code, an SMS number,
   or 10 one-time recovery codes you download and store safely.

## Using MFA

- Most sign-ins send a push notification; approve it in the CampusAuth app.
- On a trusted personal device you may skip the second factor for 7 days.
- Lab and shared machines never remember devices, so you will be challenged
  every time.

## Losing your device

If you lose your phone, sign in with a recovery code, then remove the old
device at https://mfa.example.edu/devices. If you have no recovery method
left, visit the IT Service Desk in person with photo ID; MFA cannot be reset
by email. The Service Desk extension is 4500.

Staff who handle student records must use a hardware security key as their
primary factor, not SMS.
